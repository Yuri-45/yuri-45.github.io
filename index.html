PROJECTS

________

This past semester, I had the opportunity to work on a go-to-market strategy for BreakThru Beverage of South Carolina. Tasked with gaining market share for Ketel One vodka throughout the state, a small team and I spend three months researching the brand, and formulating a campaign aimed to evolve alongside consumers: Ketel One -- The Vodka You Grow Into.

This project pushed me to approach marketing beyond the classroom and into a real-world, client-facing environment. Over the course of the project, my team and I worked collaboratively across every stage, from research and surveying to final execution. While we operated as a highly integrated team, I took particular responsibility for shaping the campaign direction, developing the strategic plan, the budget, and designing our final report and presentation to clearly communicate our vision.

Through this experience, I strengthened my ability to translate consumer, category, and brand insights into a cohesive go-to-market strategy while thinking critically about positioning, justification through data, and balancing creativity with feasibility. Presenting to industry professionals from BreakThru Beverage, Ketel One, and Diageo elevated the stakes, and the strong feedback and engaged Q&A reinforced that our strategy resonated beyond the classroom. This project showed me the impact of real-world marketing work and set a new standard for the level of rigor and strategic thinking I bring to everything I do.
